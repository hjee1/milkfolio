Concept
Life To Gather

Who are today’s offline appliance stores really designed for?Currently, when families visit together, one person browses products, another waits idly, and someone leaves having experienced nothing at all. Stores may welcome families — but they rarely remember them.

01 — Concept

A space that remembers your family

We envisioned a store that doesn’t just display products, but one that connects to each family’s everyday moments and emotions — creating memorable experiences that naturally lead to return visits and eventual purchases.

Personalized experiences are stored as family data, enabling tailored recommendations and lowering the brand entry barrier. Not just a visit — a memory our family keeps.

02 — Goals

A space that sells life, not appliances

PrimaryNo one left out

Eliminate the exclusion of any family member during product exploration by offering curated experiences tailored to each individual’s interests.

SecondaryA home built together

Collect and remember data from family interactions in the store, enabling personalized product recommendations.

DifferentiateStories over specs

A storytelling-focused environment that highlights human-centered experiences over technical specifications.

01Every family member becomes the protagonist

An inclusive UX approach that creates meaningful roles for all family members.

02Empathy-driven engagement

Recommendations built on emotion, lifestyle and personal resonance — not specs alone.

03A model built on lasting relationships

Experiences continue beyond the store — saved records, routine suggestions, product follow-ups.

04Personalized experiences from shared data

Tailored interactions that reflect each member’s tastes, combined into family-centered solutions.

03 — Service Flow

Where family memories live on

Phase 01ThinQ AppBefore visit

Enter basic info — personal details, interests and preferences — and sync household appliance usage data.

Pre-input data links to the in-store AI guide

Phase 02KioskEntrance

Each family member’s personalized data is stored and issued through the kiosk as a personal card.

Tag the card on any screen for tailored guidance

Phase 03AI ScreenIn store

As users approach, the AI display recognizes them and curates content; the transparent screen captures interaction in real time.

Detects unconscious behaviors, discomforts and points of interest

Phase 04Personal CardAfter visit

Insert the card at the end to receive insights and recommendations based on the family’s interaction data.

Reminders via app and email; data carries over to other LG stores

04 — Brand Platform
“Life To Gather” — both “Life Together” and “Life To Gather”: a life shared, and a life collected.

A brand platform that captures family moments and strengthens connections — reflecting both LG’s identity and the meaning of living together.
