“Monetization methods may change in form, but the underlying model remains the same.”In-game microtransaction systems encourage spending, but eliminating them isn’t realistic. Gamers need tools to manage — not restrict — their habits. Many young users struggle with impulse game spending; we saw an opportunity to make consumption awareness fun, not restrictive.

01 — Problem

Is there a way to reduce in-game spending?

Although there have been attempts to improve monetization models — randomized loot boxes, alternative items, battle pass systems — users’ needs remain unmet due to reduced gameplay fun, lack of instant rewards, and widened gaps between users.

“If I don’t spend money, I fall behind in the competition.”Need — personalized alternative item recommendations based on preferences and spending behavior
“I’ve become numb to spending money in games.”Need — become aware of spending patterns and make intentional decisions
“Once I start spending, I can’t stop.”Need — comprehensive management to spend more efficiently
02 — Solution

A three-part ecosystem

The app records spending externally, the device delivers real-time control and feedback, and the web analyzes and visualizes data. Seamlessly connected, these elements let users manage spending anytime, anywhere.

AppSpending tracking

Records consumption based on past actions and transfers it for analysis.

DeviceReal-time feedback

A dual-screen desktop device for in-game control, with haptic feedback that detects user behavior.

WebAnalysis & visualization

Actual vs. stated probability comparison and AI-powered, personalized feedback.

03 — App & Web
01
01 — Probability

Actual odds, side by side

Right before a purchase, the stated probability of a loot box is compared with the odds users actually experienced — and alternative items that match the player’s preference and budget are recommended in the same view.

02 — Spending Analysis

Outside spending returns to the platform

Payments made in app stores and game launchers flow back into XPense. Per-game analysis and a weekly summary show how many times — and how much — the user spent on items they didn’t need.

03 — Limit & Community

A planned spending routine, set by the user

Users set a custom monthly limit from last month’s data and AI suggestions. Experiences are collected and shared in the community — reviews, ratings and tips that connect back to smarter spending.

04 — Process

Competitively positioned, pain-driven, usability-validated

DiscoverMicro research

Analyzing and benchmarking competitors and complementors.

DefineText mining from pain points

Ideation grounded in what users actually said.

DevelopAI in usability testing

Eye-tracking tool analysis to validate the interface.

05 — Device

Feedback you can feel, wherever you play

A dual-screen desktop device brings spending control into the moment of play. The attachable console works like a joystick — 360° physical input for in-game control — and when a set limit is reached, it pushes back with vibration feedback and a decline prompt.

Detached, the console clips onto a phone, so the same spending routine continues outside the desk.
